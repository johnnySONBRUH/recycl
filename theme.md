Theme Track: We targeted the specific problem of household recyclable waste—plastics, metals, and paper—being incorrectly sorted at the point of disposal, which contaminates recycling streams and undermines recycling efforts. We would measure success in two ways: technically, using held-out image classification performance (Top-1 and Top-5 accuracy, confidence-score calibration, and inference speed) to confirm Recyclify correctly categorizes waste; and behaviorally, through a small user pilot comparing sorting correctness before and after using Recyclify, plus simulated bin-audit contamination rates and a short trust/transparency survey on whether confidence scores helped users make better disposal decisions.
